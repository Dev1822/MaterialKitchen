Meet God's Love We Deliver


Every day, God's Love We Deliver prepares and delivers medically-tailored, RDN-designed meals to critically and chronically ill New Yorkers.


Founded in 1985, they have delivered over 40 million meals and provided illness-specific nutrition education and counseling to their clients, families, care providers and other service organizations.


By The Numbers


God’s Love We Deliver is the premier food and nutrition services provider in the NYC area that cooks and home-delivers the medically tailored – and RDN-designed meals – that New Yorkers living with severe illness need.


In 2025, they delivered nearly four million meals. That's almost 80,000 meals a week, provided to more than 15,000 individuals affected by severe and chronic illness.


Our giving philosophy
